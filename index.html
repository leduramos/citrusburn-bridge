What Is Thermogenesis?

Thermogenesis is the process by which your body generates heat by burning calories. When it's working well, your body is constantly burning fat even at rest. Think of it as your internal metabolic furnace running 24/7.

What Is Thermogenic Resistance?

As estrogen declines during perimenopause and menopause, the receptors responsible for triggering thermogenesis become less responsive. Your furnace is still there — but the ignition switch is broken. No matter how little you eat or how much you exercise, you can't light the fire without fixing the switch first.
